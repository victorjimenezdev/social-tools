## Shadows
- `card`: `0 3px 8px rgba(0,0,0,0.04)`

## Typography
- **Font**: [DM Sans](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/DM+Sans) via `next/font`
- **Weights**: 400 (regular) and 700 (headings)
- **Usage**: `font-sans` applies DM Sans; headings use `font-bold` (700)
